The recommendation

Receipt. The app’s whole premise is that it quietly collects proof of spending you never wrote down, and that is what a receipt is. It is also the only one of the four that nobody would mistake for another fintech app.

The honest risk is ornament fatigue: dot leaders and a torn edge are charming on day one and can grate by week three. The mitigation is already in the mockup — mono type carries numerals and metadata only, never body copy, and the ornament appears once per screen rather than on every row.

If you want the safest daily-use choice instead, take Quiet utility. If you want the one people would actually talk about, Warung warmth is the risk worth considering — it just needs discipline to stay credible around money.
