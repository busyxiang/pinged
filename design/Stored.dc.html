What gets stored
FROM THE APPS YOU PICK
Every word the notification shows, which app posted it and when, and the details Android files it under, such as its channel. Pinged reads the payment out of it, and keeps the words so a better rule can read them again later. All of it is encrypted on this phone.
FROM EVERY OTHER APP
Not one word. Only the app's name, when it first posted, and how many notifications it has posted, so Pinged can point out a bank you have not switched on.
FROM ALL APPS TOGETHER
The time the latest notification of any kind arrived, and for each day whether any arrived at all, without which app sent them. That is how Pinged can tell you if Android has stopped it listening, and tell a day you spent nothing from a day it was not listening.
